Teem icon

We'd like to explore three different concepts for a new Teem app icon. One should develop our initial idea, while the other two are entirely up to your creative vision.

01

About Teem

Teem is a location-sharing app that helps family and friends stay connected. Today, we're focused on making live location sharing fast, simple, and easy to use.

Our vision goes further: we want Teem to become a platform where people can share places with each other, create their own maps of favorite spots, and discover maps made by others. We're starting with location sharing and building toward that future.

Please take a look at the descriptions on our pages to learn more about Teem:

02

Current Icon

This is our current app icon. It has two challenges: people sometimes confuse it with Apple Find My, and it looks a little different from most icons in our category, which tend to use a place pin. We don't necessarily think a conventional place pin is the best solution, though.

Feel free to try a variation of our current icon too, if you see an interesting opportunity to improve it.

03

Our Concept

Our sketch combines two familiar elements from the app: a place marker and a person's location indicator. It offers a starting point for one of the three concepts. We'd love you to explore and refine it, rather than reproduce it literally. You don't need to stick to blue — feel free to explore other colors.
